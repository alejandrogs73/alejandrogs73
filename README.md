## 👨‍💻 About me

**Alejandro**, Electrical Engineering student at **ESI (UCA)**. My profile is hybrid: I work at the intersection of **critical hardware**, **low-level firmware** and **self-hosted systems**.

* 🔭 **Fórmula Gades:** PCB design and development of telemetry (DAQ) systems on FreeRTOS.
* 🚀 **Asociación de Cohetería Experimental de Cádiz (ACEC):** Technical Director.
* 🔧 **R&D / Freelance:** Diagnosis and repair of complex hardware, instrumentation and radio frequency.
* 🐧 **Infrastructure:** Free Software, privacy (Tor/XMPP) and self-hosted systems (NixOS).

> ⚠️ **Note:** Most of my development activity happens on my personal Forgejo instance, not here on GitHub.

## 🔧 Experience

* **Fórmula Gades (Electronics):** PCB design for the race car and firmware development for telemetry (FreeRTOS/C++), including analysis of industrial buses (CAN/Modbus).
* **Design & Modeling:** PCB design (Altium Designer, KiCad) and 3D modeling (Fusion 360, FreeCAD, AutoCAD).
* **Asociación de Cohetería Experimental de Cádiz (ACEC):** Technical Director. Critical avionics design, solid propulsion manufacturing and systems integration.
* **R&D & Freelance:** Technician in diagnosis and repair of complex hardware. Research in high-energy physics (90kV) and vacuum technology.
* **Infrastructure:** Management of a personal server (NixOS/Proxmox) running self-hosted services (Forgejo, Tor, XMPP, Nextcloud...).
